Phrasal verbs

Los 40 verbos compuestos imprescindibles: verbo + partícula cambia por completo el significado. Escucha cada uno.

Un phrasal verb = verbo + preposición/adverbio con un significado nuevo. Give up no es "dar arriba": es "rendirse".

Phrasal verbSignificadoEjemplo
give uprendirse / dejarDon't give up now.
look aftercuidarShe looks after her grandma.
run out ofquedarse sinWe ran out of milk.
get alongllevarse bienWe get along very well.
put offaplazarThey put off the meeting.
take offdespegar / quitarseThe plane took off at noon.
turn on / offencender / apagarTurn on the lights.
pick uprecogerI'll pick you up at 6.
call backdevolver la llamadaPlease call me back.
find outdescubrir / enterarseI found out the truth.
drop bypasarseDrop by whenever you want.
break downaveriarseMy car broke down again.
come up withidearShe came up with a great plan.
go oncontinuarPlease go on.
show upaparecerHe never shows up on time.
grow upcrecerI grew up in the city.
work outejercitar / salir bienEverything worked out.
hang outpasar el ratoWe hang out on weekends.
look upbuscar (en diccionario)Look up that word.
try onprobarseCan I try on this jacket?
turn up / downsubir / bajar volumenTurn down the music.
set upmontar / configurarThey set up a new company.
bring upmencionar / criarShe brought up the topic.
carry onseguir adelanteCarry on with your story.
check in / outregistrarse / salirWe checked in at the hotel.
put onponersePut on your coat.
take afterparecerse aShe takes after her dad.
eat outcomer fueraLet's eat out tonight.
figure outdar con la soluciónI can't figure out this puzzle.
get oversuperarYou'll get over it.
hold onesperar / aguantarHold on a second.
keep onseguir haciendoKeep on studying.
look forward toesperar con ilusiónI look forward to your reply.
cut down onreducirHe's cutting down on sugar.
get uplevantarseI get up at seven.
calm downtranquilizarsePlease, calm down.
move in / outmudarse a / deWe moved in last week.
sign upinscribirseSign up for the course.
switch on / offencender / apagarSwitch off your phone.
wake updespertarseI woke up early.
put awayguardarPut away your toys.

Fíjate en los pronombres: con objeto pronombre, la partícula va después (pick it up, no pick up it). Practica más en la lección Phrasal Verbs.
