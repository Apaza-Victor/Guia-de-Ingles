Escritura práctica: el email

Escribir bien en inglés abre puertas laborales y académicas. Aprende la estructura del email formal e informal.

Email informal (a un amigo/a)

Hi Alex, Just writing to ask if you're free on Friday. Fancy going for a coffee? Let me know! Cheers, Ana

Email formal (a una empresa)

Dear Mr. Smith, I am writing to enquire about the position of junior designer advertised on your website. I have attached my CV and portfolio. I would be happy to attend an interview at your convenience. Thank you for your time. Best regards, Ana García
