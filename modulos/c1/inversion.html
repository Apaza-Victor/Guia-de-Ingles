La inversión

"Never have I seen something so beautiful." Cuando el adverbio negativo encabeza la frase, el auxiliar salta delante del sujeto. Efecto espectacular.

La regla

Never have I seen something so beautiful.

→ Nunca he visto algo tan bonito.

Not only did she call, but she also visited.

→ No solo llamó, sino que además fue a visitarnos.

Hardly had we left when it started raining.

→ Apenas habíamos salido cuando empezó a llover.

Rarely do we see such dedication.

→ Rara vez vemos tanta dedicación.

Después de adverbios o expresiones negativos (never, rarely, hardly, no sooner, not only, under no circumstances), el auxiliar (do/does/did/have/had/can...) se coloca delante del sujeto, como en las preguntas. Con to be en presente no hay auxiliar: At no time was he honest.

Disparadores comunes

DisparadorEjemplo
Never / Never beforeNever had I travelled alone.
Hardly / Scarcely ... whenHardly had he arrived when the phone rang.
No sooner ... thanNo sooner had we sat down than the show began.
Not only ... but alsoNot only can she sing, but she can also dance.
Under no circumstancesUnder no circumstances should you open this door.

Errores comunes

No uses inversión en oraciones normales ni con never que no vaya al principio. Tampoco dobles el auxiliar: Never I have seen es incorrecto; debe ser Never have I seen.
